Our Mission

Jeevan Setu (Life Bridge) is a comprehensive disaster relief platform designed to save lives and coordinate emergency response efforts during natural disasters, accidents, and other crisis situations.

We believe that in times of crisis, every second counts. Our platform connects those in immediate need with nearby volunteers and emergency services, ensuring rapid response and effective coordination of relief efforts.

How It Works

1️⃣
Choose Your Role

Sign up as a Victim, Volunteer, or Admin using Google OAuth for secure authentication.

2️⃣
Submit or Respond

Victims submit emergency requests, Volunteers respond to nearby emergencies, Admins coordinate efforts.

3️⃣
Get Help Fast

Our smart system matches requests with the nearest available volunteers for rapid response.
